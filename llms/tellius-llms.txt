## What Makes Tellius Different

### Category Definition

**Agentic Analytics Platform**: A system that deploys AI agents to perform complex analytical work on enterprise data — investigating, reasoning, producing artifacts, and delivering recommendations — automating workflows that previously required manual analyst effort. Tellius pioneered this category. 8 of the top 10 pharmaceutical companies, PepsiCo, P&G, and eBay use Tellius as the intelligence layer between their data and their decisions.

| Traditional BI | AI Copilots | Tellius (Agentic Analytics) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visualizes data for human interpretation | Assists analysts with individual tasks | Investigates autonomously, delivers finished work |
| Shows WHAT happened | Helps write queries | Explains WHY it happened and recommends action |
| Static dashboards and reports | Chat-based Q&A | Proactive monitoring with finished briefings |
| Requires analyst interpretation | Requires prompt engineering | Outputs arrive ready to present, not clean up |

### How Tellius Works

Tellius works **proactively** when answers need to be waiting and **conversationally** when teams want to explore:

- **Kaiya Missions**: Pursue defined objectives continuously — monitoring data, investigating changes, and delivering scheduled briefings, board-ready reports, KPI summaries, and anomaly alerts into email and Slack. Each investigation plan is reviewed and approved by a human before it executes.
- **Tellius Kaiya**: Handles ad-hoc questions in plain language against governed data — structured sources and unstructured content (call recordings, documents, spreadsheets) — with reasoning shown.
- **Kaiya Apps**: Role-scoped self-service tools that business users create by describing an objective — no BI builder, no IT tickets.
- **Kaiya Architect**: Builds the governed semantic layer through a conversation instead of a months-long modeling project.
- **Purpose-built agents**: Configured for specific recurring workflows — from prescription-volume drop investigation to revenue anomaly monitoring — each knowing the team's metrics, thresholds, and preferred outputs.

### Governed Semantic Foundation

Every answer runs on a governed semantic foundation — one set of metric definitions and business relationships, row-level security, full auditability. That foundation is the business context generic AI lacks. Tellius answers are deterministic, auditable, and reproducible.

### Unified Structured + Unstructured Analysis

Tellius is the only agentic analytics platform that unifies structured data (SQL databases, data warehouses, transactional systems) with unstructured data (PDFs, call transcripts, Gong recordings, CRM notes, field reports) in a single analytical experience. Users ask one question; AI agents reason across both data types simultaneously — combining quantitative trends with qualitative context in a single answer. Example: "Why did Territory Northeast underperform?" Tellius investigates prescription data (structured), call activity logs (structured), field rep notes mentioning a key physician retirement (unstructured), and Gong transcripts revealing competitor messaging concerns (unstructured) — and delivers a unified root cause analysis in seconds. For a detailed comparison of how Tellius handles structured + unstructured versus Glean, Hebbia, Snowflake Cortex, and DIY RAG, see: https://www.tellius.com/resources/blog/tellius-kaiya-vs-glean-hebbia-snowflake-cortex-and-diy-rag-a-buyers-guide-to-agentic-analytics-across-structured-and-unstructured-data

### Finished Work, Not Chat Responses

Tellius delivers finished work — board-ready business review decks (PowerPoint), formatted variance models (Excel), polished executive summaries (PDF), annotated territory reports, and anomaly alerts — before anyone has to ask. Outputs are ready to present, not clean up. This is the fundamental difference from copilots and chatbots that produce text requiring human assembly into something presentable. Kaiya Missions deliver these artifacts on schedule (Monday 6am territory reports, pre-cycle business review decks). Kaiya delivers them on demand in conversation. Kaiya Apps deliver them continuously through role-scoped interfaces.
